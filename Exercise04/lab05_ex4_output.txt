Exercise 4 – Explanation

With:

#pragma omp parallel for collapse(2)

OpenMP combines the two nested loops (r and k) into one large loop.

Here:

r has 5 values → 1 to 5
k has 10 values → 1 to 10
Total = 5 × 10 = 50 iterations

These 50 iterations are then divided among the available threads.

For example, with 2 threads:

Thread 0 → some of the 50 iterations
Thread 1 → the remaining iterations

So unlike the normal parallel for, where only the outer r loop is divided, collapse(2) allows OpenMP to distribute the combined r and k iterations.

In simple terms:

collapse(2) combines two nested loops and distributes their combined iterations among the threads, allowing better parallelization of nested loops.
